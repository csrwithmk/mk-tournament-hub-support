Privacy Policy

MK Tournament Hub · Published by CSR with MK · Last updated: September 2026

Overview

MK Tournament Hub ("the App") is a cricket tournament management tool. This Privacy Policy explains what happens to information when you use the App. In short: the App does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data with us or with anyone else.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any information at all. Everything you enter into the App - player names, contact numbers, addresses, squad lists, tournament schedules, match results, officials' names, and any other content - is saved only in the App's own private storage area on your device. This data never leaves your device, is never sent to us, and is never uploaded to any server that we or anyone else operates.

Permissions

The App does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any broad storage permission. Where the App lets you import or export a text file, it uses Android's standard system file picker, which only accesses the specific file you choose - the App is never given open access to your device's storage.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting service. It does not display ads. It does not require you to create an account or sign in.

Data Sharing

Because no data is collected by us in the first place, none is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party.

Data Security

All data described above stays on your own device, inside the App's private, app-specific storage area, which other apps cannot access. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes this data from your device. We recommend using the App's own Export feature if you want to keep a personal backup copy of your data before uninstalling or switching devices.

Children's Privacy

The App is a general-purpose tournament management tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any information from children, and as explained above, we do not collect information from any user at all.

Your Choices

Since all data lives only on your device, you are always in full control of it. You can delete individual records from within the App at any time, or remove all of it at once by uninstalling the App.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if a future version of the App adds a new feature that changes how data is handled. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at csrwithmk@gmail.com.
